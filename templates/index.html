How it works

  1. 1. Build the dev.to article

    LaunchBlast turns the brief into Markdown and submits it with tags.

  2. 2. Submit the Hacker News link

    The app logs in, fetches the submit token, and posts the URL.

  3. 3. Store the outcome

    Each response is persisted so you can open the status detail page later.
